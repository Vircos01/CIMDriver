# Car reference

This document describes the Android Auto and Automotive components of CIMDriver. The car layer contains a service, a session, utility logic, and multiple screens for car interaction.

## Scope

The car package supports a separate user experience for car interfaces. This is evident from the presence of multiple car screens and a dedicated car app service.
