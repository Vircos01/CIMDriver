# 📦 Installation: iOS (iPhone)

The current stable preview is **Version 0.0.13 Alpha** (Build 9).

This release brings the iOS app into full parity with Android Phase 8 (Fiscal Monthly Closing, Route Presets & Odometer Audit Evidence):
- Local fiscal monthly closing & share notification on the 1st of the month (100% privacy-first)
- Favorite routes as 1-tap presets in manual trip entry and 95% confidence matching during classification
- Odometer photo proof with thumbnail preview in history and visual audit appendix page in PDF export
- Consolidated multi-vehicle fleet card on the Dashboard (for ≥ 2 active vehicles)
- Work hours review list with daily triage of open workdays in the Daily Inbox
- Automatic proposals for missing departure times (via work schedule or return trip) and Dutch Arbowet-compliant breaks
- Human-readable workday status in plain language
- Target coaching with dynamic daily pace based on the active work schedule
- 1-Tap approval with reversible undo (Undo banner)
- Pre-export completeness check (`ExportPreCheckSheet`) before PDF & CSV exports
- Explainable trip proposals & automatic project codes from saved client addresses
- Human-readable tracking reliability in Diagnostics with 1-tap repair shortcuts
- Reversible changes (Undo banner) for batch classifications and approvals
- Local backup integrity and restorability verification in Data Management
- Daily Exceptions Inbox ("Daily Triage") on the Dashboard
- Confidence scoring (0–100%) & gentle single-tap review suggestions
- Trip audit & contextual details in Trip Details
- Adaptive GPS jitter filtering while stationary
- Fiscal year-end private km projection with early threshold alert
- Fiscal compliance & private detour kilometers (Belastingdienst guidelines)
- Landscape A4 PDF trip export & periodic work hours export
- RDW license plate lookup & vehicle powertrain classification (ICE, PHEV, EV)
- Live trip toggle (Business ↔ Private) during active tracking
- Calendar day tap inspection with direct trip drill-down
- EV charging tariffs & quick presets (Home, Public, Fast charging)

For more information, instructions, and a step-by-step guide for a **local installation via Xcode** (sideloading), please refer to the **[Local Installation](../ios/local-install.md)** page (found in the documentation section under *iOS > Onboarding*).
