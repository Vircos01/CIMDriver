# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Welcome to the frequently asked questions about CIMDriver. Here you'll find quick answers to the most common questions about the app.

??? tip "How does the automatic trip registration work?"
    CIMDriver uses smart location tracking to detect when you start driving and when you stop. Because of this, you never have to manually start or stop a trip again. Note: you must allow the app to use your location in the background.

??? question "Where is my data stored?"
    Your privacy is our top priority. All your trips, locations, and work hours are stored locally (on your own phone). No trip data is sent to a central server in the cloud without your explicit action or export.

??? warning "Why does the app ask for the 'Allow all the time' location permission?"
    Because the automatic registration must also work when your phone is locked in your pocket or connected to the charger in the car. Without this permission, Android or iOS can deactivate the app when you turn off the screen.

??? info "How do I export my trips for the tax authorities or my employer?"
    Within the app, go to the **Reports** or **Export** tab. Here you can choose a date range and easily export your registered trips to a clear CSV or Excel file. You can then share this file via email or WhatsApp.

??? bug "The app occasionally seems to miss a trip, what can I do?"
    Check if battery optimization is disabled for CIMDriver. Android (and sometimes iOS) automatically close apps to save battery. In the manual under [Troubleshooting](../02-runtime/diagnostics.md), you will find exactly how to exclude CIMDriver from this aggressive battery saving.

??? question "How can I delete a registered trip, address, or workday?"
    You can easily delete items by **swiping them to the left** in the list. A red trash can icon will appear, and after an additional confirmation, the item will be permanently deleted. This works for trips in the trip overview, saved addresses in the address book, and workdays in your hours registration.
