# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - iOS

Welcome to the frequently asked questions about CIMDriver for iOS. Here you'll find quick answers to the most common questions about the app.

??? tip "How does the automatic trip registration work on iOS?"
    CIMDriver uses smart iOS location tracking and (optionally) CarPlay integration to detect when you start driving and when you stop. Because of this, you never have to manually start or stop a trip again. Note: you must set the location permissions to 'Always Allow'.

??? question "Where is my data stored?"
    Your privacy is our top priority. All your trips, locations, and work hours are stored locally on your own iPhone (within the secure app sandbox). No trip data is sent to a central server in the cloud without your explicit action or export.

??? warning "Why does the app ask for the 'Always Allow' location permission?"
    Because the automatic registration must also work when your iPhone is locked. Without this permission, iOS can pause the background processes of the app, causing trips to not be registered or be incomplete.

??? info "How do I export my trips for the tax authorities or my employer?"
    Within the app, go to the **Reports** or **Export** tab. Here you can choose a date range and easily export your registered trips to a clear CSV or Excel file. You can then share this file via email or WhatsApp.

??? question "How can I delete a registered trip, address, or workday?"
    You can easily delete items by **swiping them to the left** in the list. A red trash can icon will appear, and after an additional confirmation, the item will be permanently deleted. This works for trips in the trip overview, saved addresses in the address book, and workdays in your hours registration.
