<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-car-connected: __Fully Automatic__

    ---

    As soon as you connect Bluetooth (e.g. your car kit), CIMDriver starts logging the trip. When the connection drops, the trip is neatly stopped and classified via smart location recognition.

-   :material-briefcase-clock: __Automated Work Hours__

    ---

    CIMDriver detects when you arrive at the office or a client. Workdays are automatically started, paused in between, and stopped once you're home. No more hassle with manual clocking in!

-   :material-shield-check: __Privacy First__

    ---

    All trips and locations remain exclusively local in the database on your own device, until you export them as Excel (CSV). There is no central server watching you!

-   :material-chart-pie: __Rich & Modern Dashboard__

    ---

    Insights per week, month, and year in a sleek interface. Swipe trips, view mileage allowance and quickly add notes if the system corrects your route.
